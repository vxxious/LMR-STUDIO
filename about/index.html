About LMR Studio

Our Story

LMR Studio was founded with a passion for artistry and beauty. We believe makeup is more than cosmetics; it is an expression of individuality and confidence. Our mission is to help every client feel beautiful, empowered, and ready for every occasion.

Based in Cotonou, Benin Republic, we've built a reputation for excellence, creativity, and dedication to our craft. From soft glam everyday looks to dramatic evening transformations, our team specializes in a diverse range of makeup styles tailored to each client's unique preferences and skin tone.

Our Philosophy

At LMR Studio, we believe beauty is personal. We take time to understand your preferences, skin type, and vision. Whether you're preparing for a special event, a photoshoot, or simply treating yourself to a professional makeover, we're here to make it happen.

We use only premium, high-quality makeup products and stay up-to-date with the latest techniques and trends in the beauty industry. Our team is trained to work with all skin tones and makeup styles, ensuring everyone feels included and beautiful.

Why Choose LMR Studio?

  • Expert makeup artists with years of experience
  • Personalized consultations for each client
  • Premium products and professional-grade tools
  • Wide range of makeup styles from soft glam to dramatic
  • Studio and home service options available
  • Affordable pricing without compromising quality

Meet the Founder

The Artist Behind LMR Studio

With an unwavering passion for beauty and artistry, our founder has transformed LMR Studio into a trusted destination for professional makeup services in Cotonou. Every appointment brings technical skill and creative vision together.

Specializing in diverse makeup styles from understated soft glam to show-stopping dramatic looks, our founder brings personalized attention to every client. Continuous training in the latest techniques and a commitment to premium products ensure polished results every time.

Based in Cotonou, Benin Republic, LMR Studio is known for excellence, creativity, and genuine care for each client. The mission is simple: help everyone look and feel beautiful.
