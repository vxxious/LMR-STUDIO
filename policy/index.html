Booking Policy

We operate strictly by booking only. Walk-ins are not accepted, NO REFUND after payment is made.

Working Hours

Our working hours are 9:00AM to 11:00PM.

Earlier or later bookings attract extra charges.

Deposit & Payment

A 70% deposit is required to secure your slot. This deposit is NON-REFUNDABLE.

The remaining balance must be paid on or before your appointment.

Date Changes

Date change is allowed once only.

Any further change attracts a fee.

Date changes within 48 hours attract a fee.

Punctuality & Time Charges

Please be punctual.

Arrivals later than 10 minutes attract a late fee.

Extended hours attract additional charges.

Home Service

Clients must be fully ready before my arrival.

Waiting time over 15 minutes attracts extra charges.

Studio Rules

  • No toilet use.
  • Personal items must not remain beyond 24 hours (₦500/CFA per day applies).

Visitors

Only one person is allowed per appointment.

No children allowed unless supervised.

Enquiries

Contact Lenses

Clients must wear lenses themselves.

If I assist, any outcome is at the client’s risk.
